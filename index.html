About

I work where consulting and security meet. I help technical teams and non-technical stakeholders stay aligned: turning ambiguous inputs into clear plans, tracking risks and deadlines, and reporting status in plain language.

On the security side, I'm a malware analyst at XSAV Lab. I build Python and C++ tooling and telemetry-based pipelines that automate triage and feed detection logic, and I write reports that engineering and response teams can act on.

My consulting groundwork comes from coordinating 12–14 patent application packages at once with inventors and legal teams, and from building KPI dashboards and diagnosing data bottlenecks at ProAzure. I'm a computer engineer (B.E.), now pursuing an M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence.
